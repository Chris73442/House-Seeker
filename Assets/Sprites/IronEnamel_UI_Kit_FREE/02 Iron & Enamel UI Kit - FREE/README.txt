IRON & ENAMEL — Pixel Art UI Kit (FREE edition)
===============================================
Forged iron frames, brass corner plates and glossy enamel faces. Built for RPG,
roguelike and fantasy games. Every piece is pixel-perfect and symmetric.

CONTENTS (289 PNG + font)
- Buttons (108): 9 shapes x 6 colors x 2 states (normal / pressed)
    Wide Large 63x20, Wide Medium 47x18, Wide Small 31x16, Square Large 23x25,
    Square Small 17x19, Round Large 25x27, Round Small 19x21, Pill 41x16, Hex 25x21
    Colors: red, blue, green, amber, purple, slate
    Buttons are BLANK: put any icon or text on top. The pressed state keeps the
    same canvas; the face drops 2px, so swapping states gives a real click feel.
- Icons (123): 41 icons, 9x9, in 3 variants: Light, Light_With_Shadow (9x10), Dark
- Controls (22): checkbox, toggle switch, slider track (empty + 6 colors), slider knob
- Panels (8): 9-slice window, titled window (6 colors), inset panel
- Tabs (12): 6 colors x active / inactive
- Bars (13): health / progress bar frame (empty + 6 filled) + fill strips
- Slots (2): inventory slot normal / selected      - Dividers (1)
- Font "Iron Enamel": 106 glyphs (A-Z, a-z, 0-9, punctuation, Turkish and German letters).
  TTF + BMFont (.fnt + png). Pixel-crisp at multiples of 12px. Turn off anti-aliasing.
- Spritesheets (1x + TexturePacker JSON, and 4x), nine_slice_margins.json
- PNG_1x: original size. PNG_4x: 4x nearest-neighbor (crisp).

WANT MORE? -> IRON & ENAMEL PRO (822 PNG)

- 10 colors (+ teal, rose, obsidian, silver)

  staff, bow, dagger, castle, pine, mushroom, snowflake, ghost, spider, bomb, dice,

- Editable source file

TIPS
- Scale only by whole numbers (2x, 3x, 4x) with nearest-neighbor filtering.
- Icon or text on a button: center it on the colored face; move it 2px down in
  the pressed state.
